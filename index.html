pretty early on in my secondary school years, i learnt that shit isn't permanent. stuff can disappear incredibly fast, man. one second you've got it, then the next day it's all fucking gone and you're sat there with your head in your hands wondering what the fuck just happened and where the time went. that's what drew me to photography. fuck the composition, the technique, the eye — the raw, stripped-down reason is i just wanted to capture a moment that meant something to me before it disappeared. eventually, the eye came along with it, and thank fuck it did, otherwise i would definitely not have a z6iii.

since i was young as shit, i was always kinda drawn to business. my uncle was a really successful steel tycoon in wolverhampton, and fuck, every few years when we'd go and visit him i got a little glimpse into a lifestyle i can only describe as insane. tens of rolexes in drawers, thousands in fridges, bentleys, ferraris, and whatever v8 you could imagine. ever since then, i knew i wanted to do something in business or finance. my whole family cares a lot about economics, so naturally it rubbed off on me. these days i read articles pretty much every day, i love studying economics, and fuck me, i even do the harvard online courses because i genuinely enjoy it that much. more than anything though, i became obsessed with systems — how shit works, what makes things run, and the knock-on effects that one decision can have on everything else. (neek alert, i know.)

so anyway, back to the present because we aren't living in fucking 2018, are we?

about.

this website actually started as one big bet. i was taking the piss, trying to snipe something on godaddy auctions and hoping i'd somehow end up with a funny, high-value domain for cheap. somehow i landed on 69sx.

then i realised i finally had somewhere to put all the random shit i care about in one place. photography. business. finance. coding. london. all of it. this isn't really just a portfolio anymore. it's more of an archive of the things i don't want to forget and the things i'm trying to build.

there'll definitely be a lot more photography coming soon... as long as i can actually get out the fucking house.

thanks for stopping by. it genuinely means a lot.
